Third-Party Iframes

Each iframe loads from its origin domain. Third-party cookies are written by those servers via Set-Cookie response headers — they appear under the origin domain (not this site) in DevTools → Application → Cookies. The scanner sees them via CDP Network.responseReceivedExtraInfo headers.

YouTube

Sets: YSC, VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE, __Secure-YEC under youtube.com

Google Maps

Sets: NID, CONSENT, AEC under google.com

Facebook Page Plugin (facebook.com)

Sets: datr, sb, locale, dpr, wd under facebook.com. Page plugin is more reliable than Like button on localhost/http origins. fr is triggered separately by the pixel tracking img above the iframes section.

Bing Maps

Sets: MUID, MR, SRCHUSR, SRCHD, _SS, MSFPC under bing.com
